Infidelity Recovery

Connecting with your partner after infidelity can feel impossible. The pain runs deep.

Whether it was your affair or your partner betrayed you, there is a process to recover. Private. Trustworthy. Effective.

Schedule now

Infidelity counseling is for you if you…

  • Are considering having an affair (emotional or physical)
  • Are involved in an affair (emotional or physical)
  • Had an affair that ended, has not been discovered, and you want to process the guilt or other fallout
  • Are having/had an affair(s) that has been discovered and you want to heal as a couple or individually
  • Have been cheated on and need help figuring out what’s next and how to heal (with or without your partner)

Alison’s expert guidance provides critical support & significantly shortens your timeline for healing

Research says the average timeline is 18 months. You can expect:

  • A safe space to tell your story without judgment
  • To be heard and respected, release emotion, and process pain
  • To learn how to communicate with your partner about the affair
  • To understand what contributed to the affair happening and how to prevent another one
  • To rebuild trust if the relationship continues
  • To develop a deeper bond than you had prior to the affair
