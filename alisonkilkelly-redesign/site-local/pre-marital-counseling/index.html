Your wedding is just one day.

Couples like you do pre-marital counseling because marriage requires knowledge, skills, & tools to last a lifetime.

Schedule your pre-marital coaching

Research shows just eight hours of pre-marital education decreases the likelihood of divorce by 30% because couples gain essential skills and tools for a happy and stable relationship.

Start now and learn what you need to know to enjoy a long marriage before you even walk down the aisle.

Don’t wait for problems to arise or get worse before you seek guidance.

What’s included?

Prepare for a life of love, passion, challenges, and changes!

Enjoy peace of mind on your wedding day and walk down the aisle feeling confident and genuinely excited to make a new and lasting commitment to your partner!

Comprehensive coverage of the 12 Most Vital Topics for Pre-Marital Couples

These topics determine your quality of life together and include:

  • What marriage means to you / Why get married
  • Constructive communication skills to prevent, navigate, & repair conflict
  • Expectations of yourself & each other
  • How to nourish emotional and physical connection for the long haul
  • Finances
  • Family / In-Laws
  • Children
  • Career
  • Spirituality, if applicable
  • Personal & Professional Goals
  • Relationship Dreams & Goals

Also included

  • Address concerns specific to your relationship & get feedback
  • Post-program summary of what to focus on moving forward
