Get Started
New Clients, Get Started!

3 simple steps to get started

Sessions are typically between 60 and 90 minutes. You will get a response within 48 hours.

1

Text, call, or email for availability.

If applicable, specify days and/or times that work best or don’t work for you. You will get a response within 48 hours.

Text 608.567.4648 Call Email
2

Officially secure your session with a $105 deposit.

$100 will be applied to your first session balance or kept in the instance of a late cancellation (less than 72-hours notice) or a no-show.

Please note, your appointment is not reserved until this deposit is received & date/time is confirmed by Alison.

Your credit card is NOT saved; this is a one-time charge.

Click here to hold your session time!
3

Confirm with Alison.

Confirm that your deposit was received and you are on the calendar; text/email your full name and email address to receive a link to the paperwork.

I look forward to meeting you!

When you arrive

Please let yourself into the private, cozy waiting room.

Help yourself to a glass of cold water or a cup of hot tea/coffee and relax in a recliner. Enjoy the soothing sounds of waterfalls and babbling brooks.

I will call you in when your therapy room is ready.

Meeting over Zoom?

Each session uses the same Zoom login. You’ll receive the link and passcode when your appointment is confirmed.
