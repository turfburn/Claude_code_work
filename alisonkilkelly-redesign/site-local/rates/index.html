3 reasons I do not bill insurance

01

Mandatory diagnosis

A mental health diagnosis is required to bill insurance, and this stays on your permanent health care record. Often times, symptoms do not meet full diagnostic criteria, and I do not believe this makes someone less worthy of receiving help.

02

Restricted choice & treatment

Insurance companies restrict your choice of therapist, the number of sessions you can have, the duration of each session, what is discussed in therapy, and what kind of therapy is provided. Insurance companies have no training to appropriately determine a client’s care plan, therapists do.

03

Lack of confidentiality

Insurance companies require access to your personal information and therapy notes on file. This means the details of your sessions can be reviewed year after year.
